Meet Emily

I'm an AAC specialist, but I started out like most SLPs do: with no training on complex communication needs or assistive technology. In 2015 I took over a caseload of students I had no clue how to treat. I questioned my evaluations, my IEP goals, my interventions and everything I knew about AAC. I wasn't learning on the job. I was failing.

From grant writing to lending libraries to workshops and everything in between, I took control of my caseload and fell in love with AAC. Now I help clinicians, parents and school districts with neurodiversity-affirming AAC, from staff buy-in and classroom coaching to gestalt language processing.

I share what's worked for me because I believe it can work for you, too.
