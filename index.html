Meet Emily

Communication that grows up with your learner

After years as a school-based speech-language pathologist, I kept seeing the same gap: older students with complex communication needs were too often handed a device and left without a plan. Teens and young adults deserve AAC support that respects their age, interests, and independence.

Today I work directly with AAC users and partner with parents, teachers, paraprofessionals, and fellow SLPs to build communication into every part of the day: classes, jobs, friendships, and life after graduation.

  • Age-respectful, interest-driven therapy
  • Practical strategies teams can use tomorrow
  • Focus on autonomy, self-advocacy, and real-world use

Services

How we can work together

Whether you're a parent, a school team, or a fellow clinician, there's a place to start.

AAC evaluations

Thorough assessments that look at the whole person, so the system you choose fits their language, motor access, and goals.

Therapy

Individual sessions that build vocabulary, sentence-building, and conversation skills around topics your learner actually cares about.

Consulting

Support for schools, districts, and families: IEP goals, classroom implementation, staff coaching, and transition planning.

Workshops

Engaging trainings for SLPs, educators, and families that turn AAC best practices into everyday habits.

Professional development

Workshops for SLPs, teachers & families

Bring practical, research-informed AAC training to your team. Workshops are available live (virtual or in person) and can be tailored to your setting.
